# What's new in Horizon 1.8.9

Newest first. The launcher's News page shows this file; write for players.

## A launcher of its own
- Horizon opens to its own launcher now: your account, Play, quick play with live player counts and ping, news, mods and settings.
- Updates download and build in the launcher, so the game window just plays.
- HUD cards are graphite: clearer over a bright sky, and cheaper to draw than glass.

## Vanilla menus
- Esc can open vanilla's own game menu and options instead of the glass sheets. Switch it under Settings, in the mod menu, or with Menu Style.

## F-keys
- F1 hides the HUD, F2 saves a screenshot, F3 opens the debug screen and F5 changes perspective.
- F3 with B shows hitboxes, D clears chat, A reloads chunks and H shows advanced tooltips.

## Less input lag
- Frames start just in time for the display, so the screen is about 5 ms behind your input instead of 17 to 26.

## Faster frames
- Glass blur only covers the panels that need it, and entity updates stream to the GPU, so crowded lobbies run much faster.
